M-VAVE FM-1 firmware for muscular electronic music

Bigorna is made for rhythmic noise, power noise, power electronics and IDM. It takes the Elektron Digitone II as its reference and inspiration, and tries to recreate its harshness and grit, as far as the FM-1 allows. Bigorna is Portuguese for anvil.

Features

Parameter locks
Up to eight a step, on any parameter of the sound, the track's level, pan and sends.
Six tracks
Each with its own machine, sound, octave, level, pan, steps and loops.
Four machines
4-OP, DRUM, WAVY and HIVE, chosen per track (described below). Each track keeps a sound for each machine.
Sound pages
Eight parameters a page, as on the Digitone II: SYN 1 to 4 (the machine), FLTR 1 and 2 (multimode, 4-pole low-pass, legacy, comb− and comb+, EQ; base-width filter), AMP (AHD or ADSR), FX (overdrive, bit reduction, sample-rate reduction, the sends).
MANGLE
Per track: digital destruction (wrap, XOR, bit flip, drop-outs) and a tempo-synced slicer gate.
Sound generation
HOME draws a new sound from 56 recipes — kicks, snares, hats, percussion, noise, basses, leads, stabs, drones — leaning each time towards industrial, IDM or abstract. SHIFT + PRESETS moves through the families.
Sequencer
16 steps a track, each track with its own length, clock and direction. Per step: note, velocity, length, trig condition, ratchet, MUTATE, MIDI FX, and up to eight parameter locks.
Phrase generator
Writes a phrase for the track from the role its sound plays (kick, snare, hat, noise, bass ..), with conditions, ratchets and locks; DENSITY and COMPLEXITY per track.
Loops
Up to 32 bars, three loops a track, quantized to the step and held to whole bars.
Recording
REC records the keys into the steps (velocity and length as played).
CHORD mode
Each key plays a triad in the scale: triad, inversions, sus2, sus4, open, fourths. Chords go into the steps and the loops.
BREAK
Held, the 3rd black key breaks up the track's pattern — beat repeats, half and double time, reversed windows, shuffled beats, rolls, with ratchets, flams and octave leaps; SHIFT, every track. It stays on the pattern's beat, and the loops play on as recorded. Nothing is written: let go, the pattern continues in place.
Mute and solo
MUTE mode turns the track buttons into mutes; held, a track is soloed.
Send effects
Chorus, delay (tempo-synced, ping-pong) and a plate reverb, with a send from every track.
Compressor and sidechain
A master compressor; any track can be ducked by another track's hits, from light pumping to a gate.
Patterns and sessions
16 sessions in the FM-1's flash. A session holds the whole state: sounds, effects, its 16 patterns and the loops. SAVE held saves it. The FM-1 starts with a new session, its sounds, scale and root drawn at random, or with the last session used (GLOBAL SETUP). A pattern switches in time, as the chosen track finishes its steps.
MIDI
Over USB, and in through the 3.5 mm TRS MIDI IN jack (Type A). Clock in and out, with start, stop, continue and song position; following a clock, the sequencer is held to its pulses, so it does not drift. Notes and CCs in and out on each track's channel, an AUTO channel for the chosen track, program changes for the patterns. MIDI LEARN binds a controller's knobs to any sound or effect parameter. Set in GLOBAL SETUP (SHIFT + SAVE held).
USB audio
The FM-1 is also a USB audio input: its master output, stereo, 44.1 kHz, to a computer or a phone, as any class-compliant interface, alongside its MIDI on the same cable. Tested with a MacBook Air M5 and a POCO X7 Pro (its camera app records video with the FM-1's sound).
Scales
The white keys play one of ten scales from any root.
Readable screen
Few words, very large type, high contrast, a colour per track.

Machines

4-OP

Four-operator FM after the Digitone II's FM TONE: eight algorithms, the C, A and B ratios, harmonics, detune, feedback, two modulator envelopes, key tracking.

DRUM

Three-operator FM percussion after FM DRUM: a pitch sweep, seven algorithms, operator waveforms, a wavefolder, a noise generator with grain, and transients.

WAVY

Two wavetable oscillators with phase distortion, ring modulation or sync, drift, and a noise generator (grain, tuned or sample and hold).

HIVE

A swarm of six detuned oscillators and a main oscillator at their pitch or one or two octaves below, animated, with noise modulation: pads, leads, grinding basses, drones.

Install

The installer runs in a desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera and talks to the FM-1 over USB MIDI. Nothing to download, nothing uploaded.

  1. Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable and switch it on.
  2. Open the installer, read the warning and allow MIDI access.
  3. Press Install and leave the FM-1 connected until it restarts into Bigorna.
