Try it

Bigorna in the browser, without an FM-1. Press Start: the page boots the same package the installer writes ({BUILD}), on an emulated FM-1 — its processor, screen, keys, knobs and audio.

The emulator on a page of its own, with its guide.

Playing it

Click or drag the panel as you would press and turn the FM-1's: drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it. A key held with the mouse stays held while you press another on the computer's keyboard — hold SHIFT (the last black key) with the mouse, then press 7 for SHIFT + HOME. The panel carries the FM-1's own labels; under Bigorna the six top buttons are tracks 1 to 6 (see The panel).

What to know

  • The emulator starts at the FM-1's own clock, 240 MHz. If the sound breaks up, choose a lower clock below the panel: Bigorna then has fewer cycles, and with many voices playing it may drop some.
  • Sessions saved with SAVE stay in this browser, for this build only; a new build starts empty. The emulator's full page can export them to a file.
  • MIDI in works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox (choose the input below the panel). There is no MIDI out, no USB and no installing from here: to put Bigorna on an FM-1, see Install.
  • It is an emulation: timing, load and sound are close to the FM-1's, not the same.

The emulator

The emulator is fm1-emu, by Joakim Eriksson (MIT licence), copied here unchanged with a catalog that holds only Bigorna. It is not made by M-VAVE, and Bigorna's author did not write it.
